# Daily Scan vs Triggered Rolling Deep Read

Use this when choosing or repairing the recurring reader product for Investment OS / personal-investor research.

## Core judgment

Product B 的深，不是问题更窄、预测更远，而是历史更长、比较更准、下一步更近。

A fixed stock/pair/question study can be rigorous and still fail as a recurring product. If key evidence may remain unavailable for months, a weekly reader surface will repeat `HOLD`, lose actionability, and teach the reader to ignore it. Keep that work in the backend until a current event changes the case.

## Product A — Daily Scan

Use for broad current browsing.

It answers:

- what changed now;
- what deserves attention;
- what concrete research or monitoring action is useful now.

Default shape: zero to four items, phone-readable, fewer or silence allowed. Internal fact → meaning → next-variable logic stays backstage. Reader actions may update a watchlist, check exposure, revisit an earnings/valuation assumption, open a primary source, set a trigger, or ignore noise. They are not trade instructions.

## Product B — Triggered Rolling Deep Read / 变化深读

Trigger only when a current event materially changes a tracked topic, company, sector mechanism, price path, policy path, earnings path, or risk assumption.

```text
current decision-worthy signal
→ retrieve historical facts, priors and past forecasts
→ compare this delta with the prior state and similar events
→ explain why the mechanism may be different now
→ assess the main path and at most two alternatives through the next material event
→ specify research actions, upgrade signals, downgrade signals and falsifiers
→ write back topic/thesis state
```

Default horizon is the **next material event**—next earnings, guidance, policy implementation, price print, contract conversion, data release or another disclosure that can change the read. Do not default to a six-to-twelve-month forecast or force a distant numeric target.

B may focus on one company, a sector mechanism or a connected asset cluster, but the focus is selected by the current change. It is not maintained merely because the topic was previously chosen.

## Continuous research, quiet backend

The research system can be continuous without publishing the same question continuously:

- history and thesis memory accumulate in the backend;
- dashboards wait silently for decision-changing evidence;
- no new evidence means no repeated reader update;
- a blocked topic does not own the reader slot;
- another higher-value current signal may trigger the next B;
- a new event can reactivate an old topic with its full history intact.

## On-demand Stock / Question Deep Pack

A named-stock, pair or narrow-question Deep Pack remains a valid **special-study mode** only when:

- the user explicitly asks for it;
- the decision/question is worth the research cost;
- source access is sufficient to close a useful model or comparison chain;
- the work is not being forced into a recurring cadence.

Do not treat this special mode as the default Product B reader product.

## Reader contract for Product B

A Rolling Deep Read must answer:

1. What is genuinely new now?
2. What changed relative to the historical prior?
3. What is the most likely path to the next material event?
4. What can the reader concretely research, monitor or re-check now?
5. What observable evidence would prove the read wrong?

Depth comes from longitudinal comparison and mechanism, not length, model complexity or bank-PDF cosplay.

## Operating route

```text
broad current change set → Product A Daily Scan
one material current delta with useful history → Product B Rolling Deep Read
explicit high-cost named question with sufficient sources → on-demand Stock / Question Deep Pack
no meaningful delta → no reader product; backend state only
```

Product loop:

```text
A discovers and ranks current changes
→ a high-value signal triggers B
→ B uses history to form a next-event trajectory read
→ one earned conclusion feeds later A
→ reader feedback calibrates selection and depth
```

## Failure modes

- Fixed narrow question repeated because it was once chosen.
- Six-to-twelve-month forecast used as a substitute for present usefulness.
- Missing evidence turned into a weekly `HOLD` update.
- Long article or full model mistaken for reader value.
- A news summary stretched into B without historical comparison.
- B that explains history but gives no near-term path or concrete research action.
- “Action” leaking into buy/sell/hold, position sizing, target price or execution advice.

## Acceptance

- Product A surfaces current change, relevance and a usable research action.
- Product B is triggered by a real current delta, not a fixed cadence or inherited topic.
- Product B compares against stored history/prior and names what is different now.
- The path horizon is the next material event; longer scenarios appear only when they affect the current decision.
- Reader actions are concrete but stay inside research, monitoring and exposure-check boundaries.
- Falsifiers are observable and time-bounded.
- A blocked long-horizon study stays backend rather than occupying the reader surface.
- No trade-decision language.
